# TD Capture 水彩图标

使用内置 image_gen 生成，保留透明背景；由 sips 输出标准尺寸，再用 iconutil 打包为 ICNS。

最终提示词：

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: one finished macOS application icon for TD Capture, a screen + camera recorder with a teleprompter. Primary request: WATERCOLOR style, visibly hand painted, soft pigment washes and organic paper-grain texture, with simplified bold silhouettes readable in a small Dock icon. Composition: a single centered macOS rounded-square tile on a truly transparent outer background, generous 10% margin. Within the tile, a simple ivory screen frame with two broad script strokes, overlapped at bottom right by one warm coral circular cameo with a very simple ivory human bust silhouette. Blue, lavender and teal watercolor washes form the tile. Restrained warm coral camera circle as focal accent. Flat hand-painted illustration with subtle layered paper depth, lovely irregular pigment edges and pooling. Screen and person should be immediately recognizable. No lettering, no text, no words, no desktop mockup, no glossy glass, no plastic, no metallic 3D, no photorealism, no unrelated objects. Keep it calm, editorial, tactile and elegant. Square 1024x1024 composition, transparent pixels outside the painted rounded tile.
