# TD Capture quick start

Welcome to TD Capture. This script is an example you can replace with your own words.

First, choose the screen or window you want to record. Open the camera preview, and check your microphone before starting a longer video.

Keep your introduction short. Tell your viewer what you are about to show and why it is useful.

Move through your demonstration one step at a time. Pause briefly after an important action so the viewer can follow along.

You can use the original camera background, soften it with blur, or choose one of the built-in image backgrounds.

Place the teleprompter close to your camera. Choose a comfortable text size and scrolling speed, then practice the first paragraph aloud.

The teleprompter and recorder controls stay out of the finished video. Your camera image is added to the recording separately.

For a new script, open the script library. You can paste text into the teleprompter, import a TXT or Markdown file, or send a script from an AI client through the local MCP server.

New scripts do not interrupt a recording or active scrolling. They wait until you are ready to open them.

If another editor changes a script while you are typing, your edits are kept in a separate local copy instead of overwriting the other version.

When the demonstration is complete, stop the recording and wait for the MP4 to finish. Play the file once to check the picture and sound before sharing it.

TD Capture currently exports a local MP4. Send that file using your preferred sharing app or storage service.
